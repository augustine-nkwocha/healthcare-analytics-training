# Governance Notes

## HIPAA

HIPAA is a U.S. healthcare privacy law that regulates how patient health information is stored, transmitted, shared, and protected.

Key principles:

    Protect PHI
    Restrict unauthorized access
    Minimize exposure of sensitive information
    Support patient privacy rights


## GDPR

GDPR is a European privacy regulation that governs the collection, storage, use, and processing of personal data.

Key principles:

    Lawful data collection
    Purpose limitation
    Data minimization
    Transparency
    Right to access
    Right to deletion


## PHI vs PII

PHI:
Identifiable health information connected to a person’s medical status, treatment, diagnosis, or care.

PII:
Personally identifiable information such as names, emails, phone numbers, and addresses.


## Anonymized Data

Data that cannot reasonably be linked back to an individual.


## De-identified Data

Data where direct identifiers have been removed, but re-identification may still be possible under certain circumstances.
