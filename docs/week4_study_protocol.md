# Week 4 Study Protocol

## Study Title

Association Between Household Wealth and Childhood Stunting Among Children Under Five Years in Nigeria: Analysis of the Nigeria Demographic and Health Survey (NDHS) 2018.

## Dataset

Nigeria Demographic and Health Survey (NDHS) 2018

## Research Question

Is household wealth associated with childhood stunting among children under five years in Nigeria?

## Study Objective

To investigate the association between household wealth and childhood stunting among children under five years using data from the Nigeria Demographic and Health Survey (NDHS) 2018.

## Study Population

Children under five years living in Nigeria who were included in the 2018 NDHS Children’s Recode (KR) dataset.

## Unit of Observation

One row represents one child.

## Outcome Variable

Childhood stunting.

## Exposure Variable

Household wealth.

## Potential Confounders

- Maternal education
- Maternal age
- Child age
- Place of residence

## Indicator

Stunting prevalence (%)

Number of children under five classified as stunted

──────────────────────────────────────────────

Total number of children under five

×100

## Study Design

Nationally representative cross-sectional household survey.

## Planned Analyses

- Dataset profiling
- Variable identification
- Research dataset creation
- Data cleaning
- Descriptive statistics
- Indicator calculation
- Group comparisons by wealth category
- Data visualization
- Interpretation of findings
- Public health implications
- Technical report
- Stakeholder report

## Expected Limitations

- Cross-sectional design cannot establish causality.
- Possible missing data.
- Measurement error may exist.
- Residual confounding may remain after analysis.

## Initial Hypotheses

- Children from wealthier households are expected to have a lower prevalence of stunting than  children from poorer households.
- Children living in rural areas are expected to have a higher prevalence of stunting than children living in urban areas.
- Higher maternal education is expected to be associated with lower childhood stunting.
