# Week 3B Stakeholder Summary

## Objective
To examine how life expectancy at birth changed across WHO regions between 2000 and 2021.

## Key Findings

- All WHO regions experienced improvements in life expectancy between 2000 and 2021.
- Africa demonstrated the largest improvement, gaining 9.28 years.
- South-East Asia had the second largest improvement, gaining 6.48 years.
- The Americas showed the smallest improvement, gaining only 0.23 years.
- Most regions experienced declines in life expectancy between 2019 and 2021, potentially reflecting the impact of the COVID-19 pandemic.

## Public Health Implications

Despite substantial progress, Africa remained the region with the lowest average life expectancy in 2021. Continued investment in healthcare systems, infectious disease control, maternal and child health, and socioeconomic development may further improve population health.

The decline observed across several regions between 2019 and 2021 highlights the importance of resilient healthcare systems and pandemic preparedness.

## Recommendation

- Strengthen health systems.
- Improve disease prevention programmes.
- Invest in pandemic preparedness.
- Continue monitoring inequalities in life expectancy across regions.
