# Week 4 Variable Dictionary

## Study

Association between Household Wealth and Childhood Stunting Among Under-Five Children in Nigeria (DHS 2018)

| **Research Concept** | **DHS Variable** | **Dataset** | **Variable Label**            | **Role**   | **Reason for Selection**                           |
|----------------------|------------------|-------------|-------------------------------|------------|----------------------------------------------------|
| Household wealth     | **V190**         | KR          | Wealth index                  | Exposure   | Standard DHS household wealth quintile             |
| Childhood stunting   | **HW70**         | KR          | Height-for-age-Z-score        | Outcome    | WHO height-for-age measure used to define stunting |
| Maternal education   | **V106**         | KR          | Highest educational level     | Confounder | Standard maternal education variable               |
| Material age         | **V012**         | KR          | Respondent's current age      | Confounder | Mother's age at interview                          |
| Child age            | **B19**          | KR          | Current age of child (months) | Confounder | Best measure of child's age                        |
| Place of residence   | **V025**         | KR          | Type of place of residence    | Confounder | Standard DHS Urban/Rural variable                  |                |
