# Study Planning

## Study Design

This project will use a cross-sectional observational study design based on
the National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey (NHANES)
2017–March 2020 Pre-pandemic dataset.

Because exposure and outcome are measured at the same point in time,
the analysis will estimate associations rather than establish causal
relationships.

---

## Data Source

National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey (NHANES)
2017–March 2020 Pre-pandemic.

NHANES is a nationally representative survey conducted by the National
Center for Health Statistics (NCHS), part of the Centers for Disease
Control and Prevention (CDC).

---

## Target Population

Adults represented in NHANES who meet the eligibility criteria for this
analysis.

The final inclusion criteria will be confirmed after reviewing the
available variables and documentation.

---

## Exposure

Cigarette-smoking status.

The final exposure variable, coding, and category definitions will be
confirmed during Variable Discovery.

---

## Outcome

Diabetes status.

The final outcome variable and operational definition will be confirmed
during Variable Discovery.

---

## Potential Confounders

Potential confounding variables expected to be considered include:

- Age
- Sex
- Body Mass Index (BMI)

Additional variables may be considered after reviewing the dataset and
relevant literature.

---

## Expected Sources of Bias

Potential sources of bias include:

- Selection bias
- Recall bias
- Misclassification
- Residual confounding

These limitations will be considered during interpretation of the results.

---

## Planned Data Preparation

The analysis will include:

- Reviewing dataset structure
- Identifying required variables
- Constructing an analysis-ready dataset
- Cleaning missing and invalid values
- Validating the prepared dataset

---

## Planned Statistical Analysis

The planned analysis will include:

1. Descriptive statistics
2. Exploratory data analysis
3. Estimation of smoking prevalence
4. Estimation of diabetes prevalence
5. Assessment of the association between smoking and diabetes
6. Stratified analysis where appropriate
7. Adjustment for selected confounding variables where appropriate

The final statistical methods will be determined after Variable Discovery
and assessment of the available data.

---

## Planned Outputs

The project is expected to produce:

- Dataset profile

- Analysis-ready dataset
- Descriptive statistics
- Figures and tables
- Technical report
- Stakeholder summary

## Reproducibility Plan

All analyses will be conducted using reproducible Python scripts with
structured logging, validation checks, assertions where appropriate,
clear documentation, and version-controlled project files.
